# Search growth plan — 5 November 2026

## Baseline

The Search Console query sample supplied by the founder totals 69 impressions and 2 clicks. The brand query "mentally prepare" accounts for 58 impressions and both clicks. The four nonbrand queries total five impressions and no clicks. This is a visibility baseline, not a ranking report: the date range, page breakdown, average position, and signup attribution were not supplied.

## Goal

Increase relevant nonbrand search visibility and first notes completed by eligible adults (18+) who arrive from organic search. Track signups as an intermediate step, because a signup without a first note does not demonstrate that the product was tried.

## Work in order

1. **This week: improve the existing journaling page.** Give readers a direct, usable answer to "how to start journaling" and "I want to journal but don't know how" near the top. Keep the existing one-line method, prompts, safety guidance, and product invitation. Update the article modification date and sitemap lastmod only when the content changes.
2. **This week: capture the missing baseline.** Export Search Console Performance by query and page for the last three months, including impressions, clicks, CTR and average position. In Page indexing, inspect the blog index and five articles. Record indexed status and any stated exclusion reason. Check organic landing visits, blog CTA clicks, completed signups, and first notes from the existing consent-based analytics. Do not treat unconsented visitors as zero visits.
3. **Next two weeks: strengthen discovery of existing pages.** Link the journaling article from relevant pages and share the useful guide through relevant student organizations or channels already used by the founder. Seek editorial mentions where the guide genuinely helps. Avoid paid links or mass directory submissions.
4. **After the next Search Console export: choose the next page.** Only expand a topic when queries or user interviews show demand. Candidate: "journaling when you don't know what to write" as a section on the current page, or a new page if distinct questions and enough material justify it. Avoid a separate page for every near-identical keyword.
5. **Review every two weeks.** Compare nonbrand impressions and clicks by landing page, then CTA clicks, signups and first notes. If impressions rise but CTR stays low, improve the title and description against the actual queries. If clicks rise but first notes do not, review the landing page to signup path and onboarding before publishing more articles.

## Decision points

| Signal | Likely next action |
| --- | --- |
| Article excluded from indexing | Fix the specific Search Console reason and request reindexing. |
| Indexed, few impressions | Improve internal discovery and relevant external mentions; give crawling time. |
| Impressions, few clicks | Revise search snippet to match the observed query intent. |
| Clicks, few CTA clicks | Improve the article's product explanation and invitation. |
| CTA clicks, few first notes | Investigate signup and first-night friction. |

## Guardrails

Do not promise a match, clinical benefit, or guaranteed connection. Do not fabricate user stories or credentials. Keep articles useful on their own, with the product invitation clearly separate from advice. Search ranking changes take time and cannot be inferred from a handful of impressions.
