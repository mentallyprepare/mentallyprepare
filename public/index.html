Mentally Prepare is an anonymous pen-pal journaling app for university and college students aged 18 and over. You are matched with one stranger in your region. For 21 nights you each write one honest note a day; notes unlock after midnight India time (IST), which may be a different local time for you. On night 21 both people independently choose whether to reveal their identity, and a single “stay anonymous” keeps both identities private forever. It is free during early access. It is a writing ritual, not therapy or crisis care.

The quiet problem

You are not broken. You are just not seen.

Most people your age perform fine in public. The tiredness is not dramatic. It is the kind that sits in a room with you after everyone leaves.

"What kind of tired are you that sleep does not fix?"
"When did you last feel fully seen by someone who was not trying to fix you?"

No feed. No likes. No performance. The product is deliberately small so the writing can stay real.

Trust and safety

Private by design. Not therapy.

Your words are not content. This is a writing ritual and peer connection experiment, not clinical care.

Anonymous first

No public profile, no photos, no visible college. Your identity stays hidden until you choose otherwise on Day 21.

Mutual reveal only

Identity details open at the level both people independently choose. One anonymous vote keeps both private.

Block and report

Instant block, rematch request, and moderated reporting. Read the full safety page.
