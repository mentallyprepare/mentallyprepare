The most intense parts of us are often private. Not because we are broken. Because being fully seen asks for a kind of courage most days do not give us.

Mentally Prepare is a small experiment for that courage: one honest note, one unknown person, one quiet day at a time.

Built by Anushka Kumar - for students who are tired of performing fine.

The experiment

Four quiet parts. Twenty-one days.

No feed. No likes. No performance. The product is deliberately small so the writing can stay real.

01 / 3 minutes

Answer a small emotional quiz.

Eleven sliders about closeness, distance, trust, and how you protect yourself when things feel too real.

02 / quietly matched

Get paired with another student.

Someone from another college. Anonymous by default. You meet their thoughts before you meet their name.

03 / every night

Write one note. Read one note.

A prompt opens. You write what you can. Their reply reaches you later, without pressure to perform a perfect self.

04 / day 21

Choose what becomes real.

Both people choose a reveal level. Email is never shared automatically. One stay-anonymous choice keeps both identities private.

Anonymous first

No public profile, no photos, no visible college until a mutual reveal.

Mutual reveal

Day 21 only opens identity details at the level both people independently choose.

Not therapy

This is a writing ritual and peer connection experiment, not clinical care or crisis support.

Private by design

No social feed. No follower count. No likes. Your words are not content.
