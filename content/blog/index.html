Find the words for what you're going through

College can be full of people and still feel lonely. You might miss home, find it hard to make friends, or have a lot on your mind without knowing where to put it. These guides are here for those ordinary, difficult moments. Each one offers a place to start and small steps you can try at your own pace.

If you're new to campus and unsure how to meet people, start with how to make friends in college. It looks at simple conversations, places where you can see the same people again, and invitations that don't have to feel like a big leap. If you've met people but still feel apart from them, read why you can feel lonely even around others.

Moving away can bring its own kind of ache. Our guide to homesickness in college explores why it happens and what can make a new place feel more familiar. For practical ways to build connection when the days feel isolating, try feeling lonely in college.

Sometimes it helps to write before you're ready to talk. How to start journaling offers a one-line approach and prompts for nights when you don't know what to say.

Mentally Prepare is a private pen pal journal for university and college students aged 18 and over. For 21 nights, you and a matched stranger exchange one honest note a day. On night 21, you both choose whether to reveal who you are. You can also stay anonymous. It's free during early access and offers peer connection, not therapy or emergency support.
